What does Versato’s Transport and Logistics service include?

+

Our transport and logistics service covers guest movement, vendor logistics, airport transfers and more, all delivered by our own in-house team so you get one point of contact and one clear budget.

Can you arrange guest shuttles and airport pickups?

+

Yes. We plan fleets, shuttles and airport transfers, and manage vendor load-in and venue parking, so the movement around your event runs on time and out of sight.

Is Transport and Logistics available on its own, or only with full planning?

+

Both. You can book transport and logistics as a standalone service, or fold it into full event planning. Either way it is delivered by the same in-house team, on one plan.

How is Transport and Logistics priced?

+

Pricing depends on your scale, guest count and requirements. We build a clear, itemised budget for transport and logistics within about a week of your first message, with no hidden add-ons.

Can you work within our budget?

+

Yes. We build the plan around a number you are comfortable with and then protect it. You will see a clear, itemised breakup before you commit, so there are no surprise add-ons later.

Do you travel for outstation or destination events?

+

We do. We are based in Bengaluru and plan across Karnataka, and for destination celebrations we travel with a full in-house team so the experience stays exactly the same.

How involved do we need to be on the day?

+

As little as you like. Once the plan is locked, one coordinator runs the entire day and manages every moving part, so the family can stay guests at their own celebration.

Can we book this as a standalone service, or only with a full event?

+

Either way. Book it on its own, or fold it into a full Versato celebration. It is the same in-house team and the same standard of finish.
