Event brief

Tradition, told with a modern eye.

Divya and Sachith wanted a wedding that felt rooted and unmistakably theirs, every custom in its place, but with a design language that felt current rather than borrowed.

We planned each function as its own experience, tied together by one palette and one team, from the engagement through to the reception.
