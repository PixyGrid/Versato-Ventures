What does Versato’s Cake and Confectionery service include?

+

Our cake and confectionery service covers custom designs, dessert tables, flavours tasted and more, all delivered by our own in-house team so you get one point of contact and one clear budget.

Can you make a custom cake to match our theme?

+

Yes. We design custom cakes and dessert tables around your theme and flavours, arrange tastings beforehand, and deliver and set everything up fresh on the day.

Is Cake and Confectionery available on its own, or only with full planning?

+

Both. You can book cake and confectionery as a standalone service, or fold it into full event planning. Either way it is delivered by the same in-house team, on one plan.

How is Cake and Confectionery priced?

+

Pricing depends on your scale, guest count and requirements. We build a clear, itemised budget for cake and confectionery within about a week of your first message, with no hidden add-ons.

Can you work within our budget?

+

Yes. We build the plan around a number you are comfortable with and then protect it. You will see a clear, itemised breakup before you commit, so there are no surprise add-ons later.

Do you travel for outstation or destination events?

+

We do. We are based in Bengaluru and plan across Karnataka, and for destination celebrations we travel with a full in-house team so the experience stays exactly the same.

How involved do we need to be on the day?

+

As little as you like. Once the plan is locked, one coordinator runs the entire day and manages every moving part, so the family can stay guests at their own celebration.

Can we book this as a standalone service, or only with a full event?

+

Either way. Book it on its own, or fold it into a full Versato celebration. It is the same in-house team and the same standard of finish.
