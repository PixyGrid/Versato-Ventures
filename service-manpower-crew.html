What does Versato’s Manpower and Crew service include?

+

Our manpower and crew service covers trained ushers, setup and teardown, helpers and runners and more, all delivered by our own in-house team so you get one point of contact and one clear budget.

Do you provide trained on-ground staff for the event?

+

Yes. We provide trained, supervised ushers, helpers and crew for setup, service and teardown, so guests are looked after and the day stays on schedule.

Is Manpower and Crew available on its own, or only with full planning?

+

Both. You can book manpower and crew as a standalone service, or fold it into full event planning. Either way it is delivered by the same in-house team, on one plan.

How is Manpower and Crew priced?

+

Pricing depends on your scale, guest count and requirements. We build a clear, itemised budget for manpower and crew within about a week of your first message, with no hidden add-ons.

Can you work within our budget?

+

Yes. We build the plan around a number you are comfortable with and then protect it. You will see a clear, itemised breakup before you commit, so there are no surprise add-ons later.

Do you travel for outstation or destination events?

+

We do. We are based in Bengaluru and plan across Karnataka, and for destination celebrations we travel with a full in-house team so the experience stays exactly the same.

How involved do we need to be on the day?

+

As little as you like. Once the plan is locked, one coordinator runs the entire day and manages every moving part, so the family can stay guests at their own celebration.

Can we book this as a standalone service, or only with a full event?

+

Either way. Book it on its own, or fold it into a full Versato celebration. It is the same in-house team and the same standard of finish.
