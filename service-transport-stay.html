What does Versato's Transport & Stay service include?

+

It covers guest movement, airport transfers, fleets and chauffeurs, room blocks, guest stays, parking and valet, all delivered in-house so there is one point of contact and one clear budget.

Can you arrange guest shuttles, airport pickups and hotel rooms?

+

Yes. We plan fleets, shuttles and airport transfers, and block rooms and manage guest stays, so travel and accommodation run on time and out of sight.

Is Transport & Stay available on its own, or only with full planning?

+

Both. Book it as a standalone service or fold it into full event planning. Either way it is the same in-house team, on one plan.

How is Transport & Stay priced?

+

Pricing depends on your scale, guest count and requirements. We build a clear, itemised budget within about a week of your first message, with no hidden add-ons.

Do you handle outstation and destination guests?

+

We do. We are based in Bengaluru and plan across Karnataka, arranging travel and stays for outstation families, and we travel with a full team for destination events.

How involved do we need to be on the day?

+

As little as you like. Once the plan is locked, one coordinator runs the movement and stays, so the family can stay guests at their own celebration.
