About Weddings

What a Versato Weddings feels like.

A Versato wedding is the full arc, from the first moodboard to the last farewell. We plan and run the entire celebration in-house, so decor, films, catering, rituals and coordination all move as one.

Whether it is an intimate Muhurtham or a four-day affair across Haldi, Sangeet, Reception and beyond, our team designs the look, builds it, rehearses the flow and runs the day so the family can simply be present.

How much does a Weddings cost in Bengaluru?

+

Cost depends on your guest count, venue and scale. We build a clear, itemised budget for your weddings within about a week of your first message, with no hidden add-ons.

Can you plan just one function, or the whole wedding?

+

Both. Book us for a single function like the Sangeet or Reception, or hand us the entire multi-day wedding. Either way it is one in-house team on one plan and one budget.

How far in advance should we book Versato for a Weddings?

+

The earlier the better. For peak-season dates, three to six months of runway is ideal, but we have pulled off beautiful weddings on shorter timelines too. Send your date and we will tell you honestly what is possible.

Does Versato handle the Weddings end to end, in-house?

+

Yes. Decor, catering, films, styling, rituals and coordination are delivered by our own team, so you get one point of contact, one clear budget and nobody to chase.

Can you work within our budget?

+

Yes. We build the plan around a number you are comfortable with and then protect it. You will see a clear, itemised breakup before you commit, so there are no surprise add-ons later.

Do you travel for outstation or destination events?

+

We do. We are based in Bengaluru and plan across Karnataka, and for destination celebrations we travel with a full in-house team so the experience stays exactly the same.

How involved do we need to be on the day?

+

As little as you like. Once the plan is locked, one coordinator runs the entire day and manages every moving part, so the family can stay guests at their own celebration.

What exactly is included in your end-to-end package?

+

Design and decor, photography and film, catering, styling, rituals where relevant and full on-the-day coordination, all delivered in-house. One plan, one budget, one team, with nothing farmed out and nobody to chase.
