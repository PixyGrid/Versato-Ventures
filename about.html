Two artists, one unforgettable event.

Versato Ventures is a Bengaluru event management company founded by Sourabh Kulkarni and Namratha Tejkiran. From weddings and social celebrations to cultural events, we turn a hundred moving pieces into a day that feels effortless to you and unforgettable to everyone in the room.

Our story

Not just decorators. Your whole celebration.

Mistake us for a decor team and you would be half right. Versato Ventures was started by Sourabh Kulkarni and Namratha Tejkiran, two people who could not sit still at any celebration without fixing the drape that hung a touch too low or the entry that needed one more spark.

What began as an eye for detail grew into an end-to-end event management company. Today we curate, create and execute under one roof, for weddings, social celebrations and cultural experiences alike, so you can stop juggling vendors and start enjoying your own occasion.

The Versato story

Born from the word Versatile.

Founded in 2020 by Sourabh Kulkarni and Namratha Tejkiran, two artists who chose to bring the language of art into the world of celebrations. The name Versato is inspired by Versatile: the ability to adapt, innovate and create experiences that are as unique as the people celebrating them.

Our philosophy

Every celebration is a creative canvas.

We approach each occasion as a canvas, where the memorable is thoughtfully imagined, emotionally experienced and beautifully expressed.

20+ years, combined

With over 20 years of combined artistic and event experience, we read storytelling, rhythm, design, human emotion and audience engagement. Every concept is crafted with intention. Every detail has a purpose.

The founders

Two artists, one team behind every event.

Versato Ventures is the partnership of Sourabh Kulkarni, a celebrity actor, director, anchor and theatre artist, and Namratha Tejkiran, a classical dancer. Two performers who turned a shared love of the stage into Bengaluru event design. Tap either of them to zoom in and know more.

Co-founder · Creative direction & execution

Sourabh Kulkarni

Celebrity actorDirectorAnchorTheatre artist

A celebrity actor, director, anchor and theatre artist, Sourabh brings a performer's eye to every event. He is the one walking the venue at dawn, turning a mood board into decor that actually stands up, on time and on budget. Years on stage and set taught him timing, light and the language of art, and he pours all of it into weddings, brand launches and cultural shows across Bengaluru.
