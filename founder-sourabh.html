The story

Co-founder · Creative direction & execution

Sourabh Kulkarni is a celebrity actor, director, anchor and theatre artist, and one half of Versato Ventures. Years on stage and screen gave him an instinct for timing, light and the language of art, and he pours all of it into the events he designs.

He is the one walking the venue at dawn, turning a mood board into decor that actually stands up, on time and on budget. From the first concept to the final cue on show day, Sourabh leads the creative direction and the build across weddings, launches and cultural productions in Bengaluru.

With Namratha Tejkiran he built Versato in 2020 on a single idea: bring the language of art into the world of celebrations. Between the two of them they carry over 20 years of combined artistic and event experience, and it shows in work that is crafted with intention, where every concept has a purpose and every detail earns its place.
