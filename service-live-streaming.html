What does Versato's Live Streaming service include?

+

A multi-camera setup, an HD live feed streamed to the platform of your choice, on-site screens, a recording with highlights and a dedicated tech crew, all delivered in-house.

Which platforms can you stream to?

+

We can stream privately or publicly to YouTube, Zoom, Meet, Instagram or a private link, so family who cannot travel watch every moment in real time.

Is Live Streaming available on its own, or only with full planning?

+

Both. Book it as a standalone service or fold it into full event planning. Either way it is delivered by the same in-house team, on one plan.

How is Live Streaming priced?

+

Pricing depends on the number of cameras, hours and platforms. We build a clear, itemised budget within about a week of your first message, with no hidden add-ons.

Do you provide a recording afterwards?

+

Yes. Alongside the live feed we record the full event and can deliver a clean recording and a short highlights edit after the celebration.

Will the stream be stable at our venue?

+

We plan connectivity in advance, carry backup internet and test the feed before the event, so the stream stays smooth even at outdoor and remote venues.
