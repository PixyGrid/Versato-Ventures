# Versato Ventures — Website

End-to-end event management company in Bengaluru. A static, multi-page site (plain
HTML/CSS/JS) ready to upload to Hostinger. Light beige and white theme, black footer,
your real logo, brand pinwheel colours as accents, Fraunces + Poppins fonts.

## Pages
- `index.html` — Homepage
- `about.html` — Story, **Mission & Vision**, **founder profiles with photos**, values, numbers
- `events.html` — Every function as image cards, plus a multi-day wedding timeline
- `services.html` — All **18 services as image cards**, add-ons, and the four-step process
- `gallery.html` — Film gallery (click to play) and a photo grid
- `projects.html` — Featured case study and a grid of real weddings (click to watch the film)
- `blog.html` — **Category filters**, a **spotlight / recently-published** post, and **author names**
- `contact.html` — Enquiry form (opens WhatsApp prefilled), contact cards, map and FAQ

Shared files: `styles.css`, `script.js`, `sitemap.xml`, `robots.txt`, `img/` (logo, favicon, OG cover).

## What changed this round
- **Every inner page rebuilt** to match the homepage's premium design language: dark
  page hero with breadcrumb and dual-colour headline, rich multi-section layouts, and the
  same buttons, cards and hover behaviour throughout.
- **No card without an image anywhere.** The 18 services and every event are now image
  cards; founder profiles carry photos; blog and project cards are all image-led.
- **Internal linking** added across every page (inline CTAs plus "Keep exploring" tiles
  linking to Events, Services, Gallery, Projects, Blog and Contact).
- **About** now has Mission & Vision cards, full founder profiles (Sourabh Kulkarni and
  Namratha T) with photos, a values panel and a numbers strip.
- **Blog** now has working category filters, a spotlight featured post and author bylines.
- **Contact** has a real enquiry form that composes a WhatsApp message from the fields.
- Consistent mobile menu, custom cursor, scroll reveals, count-ups and video pop-ups.
