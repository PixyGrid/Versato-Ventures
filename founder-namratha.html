The story

Co-founder · Planning & guest experience

Namratha Tejkiran is a trained classical dancer and the other half of Versato Ventures. Years of dance taught her rhythm, discipline and how to read a room, and she brings all of it to the way she plans.

She maps timelines, guest movement and every expectation so nothing is left to chance, then makes the plan on paper feel effortless on the day. From intimate ceremonies to grand celebrations, Namratha keeps every one graceful, warm and exactly on cue.

With Sourabh Kulkarni she founded Versato in 2020, named for the word Versatile and built to bring the language of art into the world of celebrations. Between the two of them they carry over 20 years of combined artistic and event experience, and she channels hers into celebrations that feel immersive, personal and unforgettable, where every concept is crafted with intention.
