About traditional events

What a Versato ceremony feels like.

Every traditional function has its own rhythm, its own rituals and its own guests. We honour each one, from the purohit and samagri to the decor, catering and coordination, all delivered by one in-house team.

Naming days, thread ceremonies, baby showers, sixtieth celebrations and housewarmings — we design the look, build it, arrange the rites and run the day so the family can simply take part.

How much does a traditional ceremony cost in Bengaluru?

+

Cost depends on your guest count, venue and scale. We build a clear, itemised budget for your ceremony within about a week of your first message, with no hidden add-ons.

Can you plan just one ritual, or the whole event?

+

Both. Book us for a single ceremony like the Naamakarana or Upanayana, or hand us the entire multi-day event. Either way it is one in-house team on one plan and one budget.

How far in advance should we book Versato for a traditional event?

+

The earlier the better. For peak-season dates, three to six months of runway is ideal, but we have pulled off beautiful ceremonies on shorter timelines too. Send your date and we will tell you honestly what is possible.

Does Versato handle the event end to end, in-house?

+

Yes. Decor, catering, films, styling, rituals and coordination are delivered by our own team, so you get one point of contact, one clear budget and nobody to chase.

Can you work within our budget?

+

Yes. We build the plan around a number you are comfortable with and then protect it. You will see a clear, itemised breakup before you commit, so there are no surprise add-ons later.

Do you travel for outstation or destination events?

+

We do. We are based in Bengaluru and plan across Karnataka, and for destination celebrations we travel with a full in-house team so the experience stays exactly the same.

How involved do we need to be on the day?

+

As little as you like. Once the plan is locked, one coordinator runs the entire day and manages every moving part, so the family can stay guests at their own celebration.

What exactly is included in your end-to-end package?

+

Design and decor, photography and film, catering, styling, rituals where relevant and full on-the-day coordination, all delivered in-house. One plan, one budget, one team, with nothing farmed out and nobody to chase.
