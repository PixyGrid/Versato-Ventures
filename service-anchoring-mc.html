What does Versato’s Anchoring and MC service include?

+

Our anchoring and mc service covers professional hosts, bilingual options, run-of-show cues and more, all delivered by our own in-house team so you get one point of contact and one clear budget.

Can the anchor host in more than one language?

+

Yes. We provide bilingual hosts so announcements and segments reach every guest comfortably, and we brief them on your programme so nothing feels generic.

Is Anchoring and MC available on its own, or only with full planning?

+

Both. You can book anchoring and mc as a standalone service, or fold it into full event planning. Either way it is delivered by the same in-house team, on one plan.

How is Anchoring and MC priced?

+

Pricing depends on your scale, guest count and requirements. We build a clear, itemised budget for anchoring and mc within about a week of your first message, with no hidden add-ons.

Can you work within our budget?

+

Yes. We build the plan around a number you are comfortable with and then protect it. You will see a clear, itemised breakup before you commit, so there are no surprise add-ons later.

Do you travel for outstation or destination events?

+

We do. We are based in Bengaluru and plan across Karnataka, and for destination celebrations we travel with a full in-house team so the experience stays exactly the same.

How involved do we need to be on the day?

+

As little as you like. Once the plan is locked, one coordinator runs the entire day and manages every moving part, so the family can stay guests at their own celebration.

Can we book this as a standalone service, or only with a full event?

+

Either way. Book it on its own, or fold it into a full Versato celebration. It is the same in-house team and the same standard of finish.
