Before you ask

Questions we hear a lot.

How far in advance should we book you?

The earlier the better, but we have pulled off beautiful events on shorter timelines too. For peak season dates, three to six months of runway is ideal. Send us your date and we will tell you honestly what is possible.

Do you only do weddings?

Not at all. Weddings are our heart, but we plan every function around them and beyond, Haldi, Sangeet, Upanayana, Naamakarana, Shashti Poorthi, engagements, receptions and birthdays.

Is everything really in-house?

Yes. Decor, films, catering, styling, purohit, coordination and more are delivered by our own team, so you get one point of contact, one clear budget and nobody to chase.

Do you work outside Bengaluru?

We are based in Bengaluru and that is home, but we travel for destination weddings. Tell us where and when, and we will work out the logistics.

How does pricing work?

Every celebration is different, so we build a plan and a clear budget around your dates, guest count and traditions, usually within a week of your first message. No hidden add-ons.
